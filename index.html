About SweetCrumbs Bakery

SweetCrumbs Bakery was founded in Nairobi with one simple goal: to provide fresh artisan baked goods every single day.

What started as a small family bakery has grown into one of Nairobi's favourite neighbourhood bakeries, serving thousands of happy customers every month.

Every loaf, cake and pastry is handcrafted using premium ingredients sourced from trusted local farmers. We believe every bite should taste homemade.

Our mission is to spread happiness through freshly baked food while supporting local agriculture and delivering excellent customer service.
